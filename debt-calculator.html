The debt-freedom ledger

Four fixed loans, paid on schedule. Then everything you can spare goes into the market. This works out when the debts clear — and what the invested surplus grows into along the way.

01 The debts you carry

Untick a debt to exclude it from every calculation below — balance, monthly payment, and payoff schedule.

02 What you already have

Money already saved and invested today. It compounds from month one, before anything you add.

€

03 What you can set aside

Add a row for each stretch of time. Each says: from which month to which month (month 1 = now), and how much per month you can spare on top of the loan payments. Leave gaps if some months have nothing spare.

04 When can you stop & pay it off

The moment your invested pot first grows big enough to clear the debt still remaining. From that month you could stop saving and wipe the balance in one payment. Faster returns reach it sooner.

05 If instead you keep saving the whole time

What the pot becomes if you run every savings period to the end, at each annual return, compounded monthly.

Debt model (your rule): each month, half of every loan's fixed payment reduces its principal; interest is treated as the other half and not modelled separately. Investment contributions and starting balance compound monthly. Figures are estimates — real returns vary and are not guaranteed. This is not financial advice.
